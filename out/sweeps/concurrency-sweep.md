World: 50 goodwill obligations (25 of 15000 OVERNIGHT_STRANDED, 25 of 20000 MOBILITY_ASSIST; demand 875000), one grant of 525000 (60% of demand). N workers drain one shared queue of the 50 proposals, shuffled with a per-repetition seed (recorded in the JSON), each calling the gateway's `propose()` on its own database connection. N = 1 is the sequential control. Every repetition has a fresh in-memory replica set. The workers are concurrent async callers inside one Node process, not separate OS processes.

Conditions checked per run: (1) held + settled <= grant at the end and in every sample of a poller reading the grant document throughout; (2) replaying INTENT_RESERVED events in `_id` order the running total never exceeds the grant; (3) `held_minor` equals the sum of the goodwill intents; (4) one intent per effect key, and all 50 proposals answered RESERVED or AWAITING_BUDGET; (5) no held amount without its intent; (6) no proposal left awaiting budget would still fit; plus the gateway's invariant scan (P1, P2, P3, P9).
